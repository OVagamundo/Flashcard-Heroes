This document serves as the authoritative refactor guide for Flashcard Heroes. While the core identity remains a deterministic roguelike auto-battler powered by an SRS Flashcard engine, the Leveling Up system acts as the primary catalyst for the following structural shifts.  1. The Leveling CatalystLeveling up is the mechanical driver used to create a "Power Curve" that rewards investment without inflating the token economy.  Identical Unit Leveling: Leveling is strictly reserved for combining identical units (e.g., two Tier 1 "Fire Imps").  Ability Scaling: Each level increase upgrades the unit’s unique ability in addition to increasing raw HP and PWR.  Leveling Pathways: A Level 3 unit can be achieved by merging two Level 2 units, or a Level 2 unit with two Level 1 units, ensuring cumulative investment is preserved.  Cost Efficiency: Leveling up increases power while strictly preserving the unit's original Tier and Token Draw Cost.  Partial Leveling (Lv 2.5): Adding a third identical unit creates a "Level 2.5" state, providing additive stat gains without triggering the Level 3 ability upgrade until the fourth unit is added.  2. Strategic PersistencePersistence is applied across tactical and strategic layers to ensure player choices have lasting impact.  In-Battle Persistence: Merges and Level-ups achieved during the Management Phase are permanent for that specific encounter. Units do not reset to base stats upon death or when reshuffling from the Shared Discard Pile.  Run Persistence: Level-ups and merges performed in the Run Inventory are permanent for the entire run.  Specialized Leveling Nodes: Permanent leveling in the Run Inventory is restricted to specific Leveling Encounters rather than standard shops or rest sites.  3. Economic Metric: Unit CostThe game utilizes Unit Cost (the Gold value of the base unit) as the primary metric for calculating power and AI scaling.  Valuation Logic: The economic value of a unit is calculated as:$$C_{Unit} = \text{Tier Gold Cost} \times \text{Level 1 Equivalents}$$  Economic Parity: A Tier 1 Level 3 unit (Cost: 1 Gold $\times$ 4 units = 4) is economically equivalent to a Tier 3 Level 1 unit (Cost: 4 Gold $\times$ 1 unit = 4).  Additive Stats: During any merge or level-up, stats are strictly additive ($HP_A + HP_B$), remaining within the global 99 HP/PWR cap.  4. Simplified Equipment: One-Item ConstraintTo maintain tactical clarity, the equipment system follows a strict limit.  Single Slot: All units are restricted to one item slot regardless of Tier or Level.  The "Base Unit Wins" Rule: When units are merged or leveled, the item currently equipped on the "Target Unit" (the unit already on the board/bench) is kept.  Item Discard: The item on the "Dragged Unit" is unequipped and sent specifically to the Shared Discard Pile.  5. AI Scaling & Adaptive GenerationThe Encounter Generator and AI abilities utilize the same budget-based scaling to challenge player engines.  Generator Priority Hierarchy:Quantity (Primary): The generator first attempts to fill all available lineup slots with units.  Weighted Balance: Remaining budget is distributed with equal weight across Tier, Level, and Items.  Budget Flexibility: If an enemy ability has a budget to spawn units but the lineup is full, the AI uses that budget to level up existing units or equip items instead.  Progression: The Encounter Budget increases daily, with Boss and Elite nodes receiving higher multipliers.  6. The Drafting Reward SceneThe post-battle sequence is a restricted resource management phase.  Performance Limit: Players are permitted only one flashcard session per reward scene to generate their Token budget.  The Tray & Sell Area: Drawn GachaBalls enter a tray; from there, they can be added to the inventory or dragged to a Sell Area.  Resale Rates: Tier 1 units sell for 1 Gold; higher tiers/levels sell for 50% of their Unit Cost (e.g., a Tier 3 unit valued at 4 Gold sells for 2).  Auto-Collect: Clicking "Leave" automatically adds all balls in the tray to the Run Inventory, triggering a Random Eviction if the 39-slot capacity is exceeded.
